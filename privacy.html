Information we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, organization (if given), project type, and message. We do not collect this information through any other means on this site.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard technical data for security and reliability purposes, such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential project.
  • To keep a record of our conversation if we move forward with working together.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

Contact form submissions are delivered to our inbox using Resend, an email-delivery service. Resend processes the contents of your submission solely to deliver it to us. This site is hosted on Vercel.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or, if we begin a working relationship, for the duration of that relationship and as required for our business records.

Your choices

You can request that we delete information you've submitted by emailing us at the address below.
