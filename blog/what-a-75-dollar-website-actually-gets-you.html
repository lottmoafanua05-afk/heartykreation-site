What a $75 website actually gets you

Seventy five dollars sounds either too cheap to be real or too cheap to be good. Here is exactly what it is and how it is possible.

What you get

One page, built from your answers, with the five sections that do the work: a hero that says what you do and where, your services, who you are, proof from real customers, and a contact section with a tappable phone number and a form that actually reaches you. Your domain connected, a share image so links look right, and a page that loads fast on a phone.

Live within twenty four hours of your domain connecting. Revisions included.

Why it can be that fast

Because the structure is already decided. We are not designing a page from scratch for every customer. You pick a style from the catalog, the layout stays the same underneath, and what changes is your content and your look.

Think of a print shop. The press is already set up. Your design goes on the shirt. Nobody rebuilds the press for every order, and that is why a shirt costs what it costs.

The cost we cut is design time, not care. Every build still gets checked before it reaches you.

Who it fits

  • Businesses with no website at all, losing anyone who searches for them
  • Businesses whose website is a Facebook page
  • New businesses that need to look real before they can charge real prices
  • Owners who want a presence without a monthly platform bill and a login they will never use

Who it does not fit

If you need online booking, payments, a store, a customer login, or a dozen pages targeting different searches, this is the wrong tier and we will say so. Buying a starter site and then asking it to be a booking system helps nobody.

What you own

The domain goes in your name, always. The site is a plain page, not something trapped inside a builder account, so if you ever leave, you leave with it. That is deliberate. A customer who stays because leaving is painful is not a customer, they are a hostage.

When you have outgrown it

Three signs: you keep getting asked questions the page does not answer, you want to be found for more than one thing, or you are typing the same reply to inquiries every week. That is when a multi page site or a real tool starts paying for itself.

Until then, one good page beats the site you have been meaning to build for two years.
